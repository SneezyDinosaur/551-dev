## Match Conditions
  - Two teams spawn on separate airbases roughly 80nm apart. With the objective of eliminating every member of the enemy team.
  - Teams will have 10 minutes to engage with each other freely across the map at any location most advantageous to them. After the 10 minute mark of a round, teams will have to remain within 10nm of the bullseye or they will lose the round.

## Draws
  - If all of one team is eliminated, there is a 15 second extension of the round. During which the round cannot end. If all members of both teams have no living pilots BEFORE THIS 15 SECONDS IS UP, the round win goes to BOTH teams. A draw CANNOT end a match. If one team is on match point and a draw occurs. The point goes to the team not on match point, up to a "sudden death" round. If a draw occurs on a sudden death, another round must be played.

#Match Organization/Scheduling

  - Teams may reschedule their match if the agreed time is unfavorable or prevents a full roster. (see below)
## 1: Match Delays
  - A match can be delayed no longer than 30 minutes from the original start time. The team that is unready to play will automatically forfeit the round. More than one occurrence of this per Episode will place the team subject to penalty up to/including DQ from Episode. This is case by case reviewed by @Tournament Organizer or @Trusted Users . Exceptions can be made in event of emergency.

### 1.1: Short-roster games.
  - If one team is short players, matches can be played with no less than 4 jets per team.
  - The decision to play one aircraft down (and which aircraft is being down) is up to the discretion of the prepared team to accommodate the request of the unprepared team.
  - It is the responsibility of the Captain and their team to communicate and organize subs if they are short players after committing to a scheduled time.

## 2: - Reschedule Confirmation
  - A Tournament Organizer must receive proof that both teams agreed to the reschedule before the original and new times. Preferably in #captains or the chat the game was agreed to be scheduled in.
###  Acceptable proof:
  - One team states reschedule in chat, other agrees in written text.
  - Screenshot of DMs showing agreement.
  - Teams should confirm 24 hours in advance.
Violation: Failure to inform Tournament Organizer voids reschedule.

## 3: - Reschedule Restrictions
  - Matches cannot be rescheduled to start less than 5 hours before the latest score reporting deadline (see #4).
  - No limit on how early a series can be scheduled if both teams agree.
Violation: Illegal reschedule times void the reschedule.

## 4: - Default Time
  - If teams cannot agree on a reschedule time, the match stays at the last rescheduled time.

## 5: - Score Reporting
  - Both teams must report scores, including streamed/casted matches in #match history.
  - At least one team must report within 4 hours after the match completion.
  - Protest of results must happen within 36 hours of match completion. After this time, protests are void, and the original match result is considered valid.
Violations:
  - No report within 4 hours = minor warning for both teams.
  - Multiple no-reports of match results = review by Tournament Organizer for potential disqualification from Episode and forfeit of match if applicable.

### 5.1: - Exceptions to Score Reporting Deadlines
  - School breaks recognized by Tournament Organizer may delay deadlines and play weeks.
  - Extensions may be granted case-by-case if Tournament Organizer is notified in advance.
